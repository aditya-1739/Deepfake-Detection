About

This project aims to combat the growing threat of synthetic media and misinformation using state-of-the-art artificial intelligence. By providing an accessible and highly accurate verification tool, we empower individuals and organizations to trust their digital content.

Our Technology

EfficientNet-B0 Core

A lightweight yet powerful convolutional neural network fine-tuned on the FaceForensics++ dataset to detect pixel-level manipulation artifacts.

MTCNN Face Extraction

Multi-task Cascaded Convolutional Networks accurately isolate facial regions across frames to ensure the model focuses only on relevant biometric data.

Temporal Sampling

For video analysis, we extract and evaluate multiple frames across the timeline to detect temporal inconsistencies often missed in single-frame deepfakes.

ONNX Runtime

The model is exported to ONNX and served via a high-performance inference engine, guaranteeing low latency and efficient hardware utilization.
